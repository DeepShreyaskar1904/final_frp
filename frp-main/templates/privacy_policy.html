Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Welcome to Faculty Review Portal. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a faculty review, we may collect information such as your name, email address, review content, and information provided through the review form.

2. How We Use Information

Information submitted through the portal is used to manage, analyze, and display faculty feedback and to improve the educational experience.

3. Email Communication

The portal may use Gmail API services to send email notifications related to submitted reviews. Google account authorization is used only for the configured email-sending functionality.

4. Google Account Access

If Google authorization is used, the application requests only the permissions necessary for its configured functionality. We do not sell or use Google account information for advertising.

5. Data Protection

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information stored by the application. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

6. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services such as Google services, Render, and database hosting providers to operate the website and its functionality.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to operate the faculty review system and maintain its records.

8. Your Choices

If you have questions about your information or would like to request clarification regarding data handling, please contact the application administrator.
